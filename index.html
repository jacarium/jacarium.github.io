Features

Everything for the work, in one place.

  • Notes

    Write fully offline with live preview and code highlighting. Start from your own templates and export to PDF or Markdown.

  • Troubleshooting

    Load an sosreport or gather-script output and see the server or Kubernetes cluster laid out visually.

  • Tools

    More than 50 tools for string manipulation, time conversion, encoding and much more.

  • Tasks

    TaskWarrior, built in and linked to your notes. Add and update tasks with the /task command without leaving your notes.

  • Workflows

    A visual scripting language inside your notes. Build and run workflows from your notes using the /wf command.

  • Jira search

    Build JQL queries and search Jira for existing tickets. Save the queries you reuse as templates.

  • Calendar

    Connect Google Calendar and create meetings straight from the documents page, with templates.

  • Kanban

    Boards to track your progress, right next to your notes and tasks.

  • Games

    Quizzes and more games to test your knowledge and your friends1.

  • AI assistant

    Bring your own LLM. With a local model, load your notes into a vector database and ask questions about them.

Privacy and data

Your notes stay on your infrastructure.

Jacarium is offline by default.

Where your data lives
In your browser first. With Home Server on Raspberry Pi.
What we collect
Nothing.
Export and backups
Export to Markdown, ZIP, JSON or PDF at any time.

Important

  • For access away from home, connect to your home server through your own VPN.
  • Traffic is encrypted, but stored data isn't, so use disk encryption such as FileVault or LUKS.
  • You run and secure it yourself, without warranty under the MIT licence.
